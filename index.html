# Leadership OS — Premium Starter Kit A self-guided, rigorous version of Leadership OS. No project setup, no configuration. Drop two files into a fresh chat, say the word, and it guides you through the whole process. ## How to run it 1. Open a **new chat** in Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini. 2. Drop in (paste or upload) **both files**: `LEADERSHIP_OS_ENGINE.md` and `LEADERSHIP_OS_REFERENCE.md`. 3. Type: **Let's start Leadership OS.** That's it. It introduces itself and walks you through everything — checking what inputs you have, auditing your behavioral evidence *with you*, analyzing against the nine constructs, challenging its own conclusions, and producing four documents (Leadership Profile, Working-With-Me Guide, AI Calibration Document, Development Roadmap). ## What makes this different from the free Starter Kit - It **audits real behavioral evidence** visibly — showing you the evidence, alternative readings, and confidence for each pattern, and asking you to confirm before anything counts. - Its development roadmap **reasons from established theory**, tied to your specific evidence — not generic advice. - It **argues with itself** and downgrades its own confidence where the evidence is thin. - It's a **guided conversation**, not a prompt you run yourself. ## About your behavioral evidence (the corpus) The richest runs use your real history. When it asks, you can: - **Export your AI chat history** (ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all offer a data export) and upload the file, or - **Paste a good sample** of real conversations, work documents, or writing. - If your model has **memory / cross-chat history turned on**, it may surface patterns from your broader history — but it will always show you the evidence in a table and let you confirm or strike each one. It will never rely on anything you can't see, and it will never invent evidence that isn't there. Redact freely — nothing confidential or about people who haven't consented. It only needs to see *how you think*. ## Your brake pedal If a run ever drifts — a score appears, clinical language creeps in, or it jumps ahead — type **GOVERNANCE CHECK**. It will stop, re-check itself against its own rules, retract anything off, and tell you where you're resuming. ## Signs something's off (restart if you see them) A score/grade/percentage; "diagnostic" or "unconscious" language; a corpus audit when you gave it no material; deep conclusions before it walked you through your inputs; all-glowing output with no downgrades or "where this is probably wrong." Almost always it's worth a fresh chat and a clean re-drop of both files. ## Privacy Everything stays in your chat, in your own account. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
